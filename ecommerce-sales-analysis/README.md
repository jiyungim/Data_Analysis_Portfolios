# E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes 5,000 e-commerce sales transactions using Microsoft Excel to evaluate sales performance, customer behavior, regional performance, and payment method usage.
The analysis focuses on data validation, KPI development, PivotTable analysis, and dashboard visualization to identify key sales patterns and business insights.

## Tools
- Microsoft Excel
  - PivotTables
  - Formulas
  - Data Validation
  - Charts
  - Dashboard Development

## Skills & Techniques
- Data Cleaning & Validation
- Data Quality Checks
- KPI Calculation
- SUMIFS & COUNTIFS
- PivotTable Analysis
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Data Visualization
- Dashboard Development
- Business Insight Identification
 
## Key Insights
- Electronics generated the highest revenue and order volume among all product categories, with $1.83M in revenue across 1,777 orders.
- Beauty had the highest average order value at $1,059.28 despite having the lowest order volume.
- The West region recorded the highest revenue, order volume, and average order value, generating $1.35M across 1,289 orders.
- Card was the most frequently used payment method, accounting for 45.4% of all orders.
- Overall customer rating averaged 2.97 out of 5, indicating potential room for improvement in customer experience.

## Business Recommendations
- Electronics represents a key sales category based on both revenue and order volume, while Beauty demonstrates stronger order-level value despite lower sales volume.
- The strong performance of the West region suggests an opportunity to further evaluate regional customer demand and sales strategies.
- Card usage accounted for nearly half of all transactions, highlighting its importance as the primary payment method.
- The relatively low average customer rating suggests that further analysis of customer experience factors could help identify opportunities for improvement.
